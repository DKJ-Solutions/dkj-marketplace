# Release notes v6.7.0 🚀

**Date:** 2026-11-04\
**Type:** Minor\
**For whom:** consumers of this product

The Toolbox gets a desktop shell, a toast reminder and latest-run Stopwatch cards

## What changed

No action needed for any of these. Update the plugins and your Toolbox picks them up on its next start.

### A desktop Toolbox

The Toolbox now looks like a desktop app rather than a website. A thin bar shows where you are, a side tree
lists every Toolbox page so you reach any of them in one click, and the main pane carries its title in a
compact toolbar. The tree and the pane scroll on their own. Your product's own pages keep their mobile-first
layout, so nothing changes for the people who use them.

### A toast for airsquats

The airsquats reminder now rises as a toast in the bottom-left corner when squats come due, and sinks away
once you tick them off. It floats over the page, so your header no longer jumps down each hour.

### Stopwatch shows your latest run

Each card on the `/stopwatch` overview now shows your label's latest run, with its change against the run
before it (`+` faster, `-` slower), not the fastest run ever. A run that got slower no longer hides behind an
old record. A run still going shows its live clock, and the slowest labels come first.
