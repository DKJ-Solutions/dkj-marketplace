# Release notes v6.16.0 🚀

**Date:** 2026-11-14\
**Type:** Minor\
**For whom:** consumers of this product

## What changed

Updating the plugins (`update-plugins`) is all most repos need to do. Two kinds of repo have follow-ups:
an app with its own styling layer on top of the brand book renames a few retired tokens, and a BWJ store
can take two new board automations. Both are below, with exactly what to do.

### Rename retired brand-book tokens

The brand book was trimmed to fewer, clearer steps. **If your app's own domain layer uses any of these
names, rename them.** The house-style gate names every use you missed, so you can update first and let
it point you at what is left.

| retired | use instead | what changes on screen |
|---|---|---|
| `--radius-1-5` | `--radius-2` | control corners 6 → 8px |
| `--radius-2-5`, `--radius-4` | `--radius-3` | card and dialog corners 10 or 16 → 12px |
| `--fg-2`, `--fg-3`, `--fg-4` | `--fg-1`, or `--primary-muted` for quiet text | text a shade stronger |
| `--text-title` | `--text-h2` | nothing: same size |

The radius scale is now one step per role: `--radius-1` (4px) for a small element, `--radius-2` (8px)
for a control, `--radius-3` (12px) for a container. The migration table is in the brand book README.
An app with no domain layer of its own has nothing to rename.

### BWJ stores: two board automations

- **Every new Asana card gets its Prio-Score.** Add `Get-AsanaPrioFieldGid` to
  `scripts/repo-config.ps1`, naming your board's `Prio-Score` field, and `report-issue` scores each
  card from its urgency line as it creates it. The issue then carries its `prio-N` label the same day.
  Without the seam nothing changes.
- **Cards stay in progress while their issue is open.** Copy the new `asana-open-section` workflow (your
  session check asks for it), and a card dragged back to `1. Todo` while its issue is open returns to
  `2. In Progress` the next morning.

Also for stores, and needing no action:

- **A reach label now needs a reason.** Labelling an issue `minor` without a `## Why minor` section
  (who notices, and what) is stopped, and you are shown the section to write.
- **A new session-start error names reach-labelled issues without a card**, until `report-issue` has
  made one for each.
- **No more false errors at session start** for files that the same session start was about to bring
  in.
- **`/issues-sweep-reviews` ships Asana-mirrored issues on the first try**, instead of every one being
  refused once.

### Your pages look different

No action needed for any of these: they come with the stylesheet.

- **All text is set in Inter**, the same letters on every device and in Figma, instead of each device's
  system font. Admin has it at once. An app that copies the brand book in takes it by copying
  `InterVariable.woff2` and its license next to `house-style.css`. Without the file the system font stays,
  and nothing asks for it.
- **Phones get a back button instead of a breadcrumb.** Below 48rem the site header shows a back
  button one level up and the page's name, and the breadcrumb bar is hidden. If your app draws its own
  site header, give every page below home the back cell and title in the same update, or its phone pages
  lose their way up.
- **Headings are smaller on a phone** (760px and narrower): `h1` 36 → 28px, `h2` 20 → 18px, so more of
  a page fits on the first screen.
- **The page title is heavier, and notes and meta lines are quieter**, matching the Figma library again.

### More from the team

- **A new parking label, `awaiting-session-dev`**, for an issue that can be worked on but is too large
  for one sweep turn. A sweep parks it with the steps still to do, instead of leaving it looking
  skipped. Your session check reports the label missing until you run `adopt-triage-labels`.
- **A content product may open a page with a short lead.** A product built as a reader's briefing can
  put one or two plain sentences under a summary page's `h1`, once its repo states
  `Content product (brand-book rule 5): yes.` in its facts rule.
- **One line fewer at every session start**: the dispatcher's timing line is gone.
