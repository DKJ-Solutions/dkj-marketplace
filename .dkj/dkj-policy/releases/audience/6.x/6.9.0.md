# Release notes v6.9.0 🚀

**Date:** 2026-11-06\
**Type:** Minor\
**For whom:** consumers of this product

Admin gains a Goals page and a search field, and the brand book splits into core and product

## What changed

Two items may ask something of you, so start with those. Everything else needs no action: update the plugins
and your next session picks it up.

### Action: link the product stylesheet

This applies only if your DKJ-Solutions product repo vendors the brand book. Add `house-product.css` beside
your copy of `house-style.css`, and link it after the core. The product's site header, breadcrumb bar, forms
and dialogs now live in that file. Without it they lose their styling as soon as you take the new core. A
repo that renders only Admin, such as every BWJ-Development repo, needs nothing.

### Action: one flag for merge-on-green

This applies only if you adopted merge-on-green before #3810. Re-run `adopt-ci-floor`, and it names your
runner under `[settle]`. Append `-WaitForSettleSeconds 660` to the picker line it shows. After that, the last
pull request to go green merges within minutes instead of waiting hours for the schedule.

### Plan big goals on Admin

You can now label an open issue `goal` and see it on Admin's new **Goals** page (`/dev/goals`). Its
milestones are its sub-issues, and their tasks are theirs. The page always shows one **Now** card, the next
step to take, so on a project too big to see the first step of, Admin names it. The new `plan-goal` skill
breaks a goal into milestones and tasks with you, and files them once you agree. The page moves as issues
are claimed and closed, so you never keep a second list.

### Jump to any Admin page

Press Ctrl K (Cmd K on a Mac) anywhere in Admin, type part of a page's name, and press Enter. It finds every
page, including the ones under Rules, Skills and Stopwatch.

### Your own objects in Admin

You can now put your repo's own data (tracks, recipes, playlists) at the top of Admin's navigation, each with
its count as a badge. State `Get-AdminObjects` in `scripts/repo-config.ps1`. A repo that states nothing sees
no change.

### Review passes ship everything

`/issues-sweep-reviews` now ships the branches you approve, where it used to refuse every one of them on its
plan. In a repo with two owners, either of you can approve and ship the other's parked branch, and the
approval names whose work it was.

### Smaller things

- Admin's overview pages show one compact table per section instead of a grid of cards.
- The airsquats reminder has a close button, so you can put it away without ticking off squats you have not
  done.
- A session driving Chrome can now open the Admin and product pages it built, to look at them before handing
  them to you.
- When you ask about your own stuck work and the session finds a fix it can carry out, its answer ends by
  offering that fix. It still acts only after your yes.
- Admin and product pages can no longer be framed by another page.
