# Release notes v6.15.0 🚀

**Date:** 2026-11-13\
**Type:** Minor\
**For whom:** consumers of this product

The brand book's colours are computed per theme and every series reads in every theme

## What changed

Updating the plugins (`update-plugins`) is all most repos need to do. A BWJ store repo has one follow-up:
copy the `asana-closed-message` template again (below). Everything else takes effect on its own, and
much of it is visible: your pages, charts and Admin look different the moment you update.

### BWJ stores: copy one template again

The Asana closed-message workflow no longer tells a requester an issue is "back in development" when two
events on one issue arrive out of order and the issue is in fact closed. The fix lives in the template
your repo copied, so it reaches you only once you **run `adopt-bwj-development` again** to copy it.

Also for stores, and needing no action:

- `build-golive-block.ps1 -Hotfix` writes the go-live block for a change that ships through your hotfix
  lane: it says the change goes live once the requester approves it, with `-LiveBy <date>` where they have
  a deadline, instead of naming a release day you would have to bend.
- The session start no longer prints `closed-message-sessioncheck skipped due to an error`, and an
  Asana-linked issue left open after its `-NoResolves` merge is reported again.

### Your pages look different: the brand book's colours

- **Section headings take the text colour.** `h2` and `h3` move from the theme colour to `--fg-2` and
  `--fg-3`. The theme colour is now the accent alone: `h1`, links, the main button and states.
- **Controls show their edge.** A plain button, a segmented control, the info button, the search and
  filter fields, and the product layer's stepper and combo field draw a border in `--bg-5`, a grey of the
  theme's colour that reads at 3:1 on the page and on a card. If your own domain layer draws a control,
  give it `--bg-5` for the same edge.
- **Chart colours are chosen per theme, and they read in every theme.** `--graph-1` to `--graph-3` no
  longer look like the theme's own colour, a warning or a gain. In the dark themes they are lighter, so a
  series stands out clearly against the page instead of sinking into it. In the pink, lilac, sky and
  orange themes the three series are a blue, a teal and an aqua. The light themes keep their series.
- **Every theme colour and text colour now reads on the page** at 4.5:1 or better, in all twelve themes.

No action needed: the colours come with the stylesheet, and none of the token names you use has changed.

### Admin follows its new design

- **A new layout**, built from Figma wireframes: a full-height sidebar with the repo's name at the top and
  Settings at its foot, and every page opening on a page header. Home lists your pages in a framed table,
  Goals shows each goal as a card with its one next step and its milestones, and Today shows the day goal
  beside the week. An empty Goals or Today page says what will appear and gives you one button that copies
  the prompt to fill it.
- **A Progress page** beside Goals. It shows how far each milestone is (built, used or measured) and how
  the work ran: the assignments, how long Claude worked, and how long it waited on you, including the time
  you spend reading and deciding before each prompt. Questions asked and steps done by hand are counted too.
- The brand book's Admin shell reference shows the same structure, so a page you build from it matches
  what Admin draws.

### More from the team

  Chris reads it wherever you work: how long a question should be, who tests, how a result is shown. It
  never loosens the safety rules. The template is on
  [`working-profile.md`](../../../../../plugins/dkj-subagents/dkj-subagents-alpha/skills/orchestrator/working-profile.md).
- **Uma builds a Figma wireframe the way a UX designer does**, without being asked: tokens and text styles

- **`/measure-decision-stops`** shows how often sessions stopped to ask you a menu question, and how often
  you took the recommended option unchanged. It costs nothing until you type it.
