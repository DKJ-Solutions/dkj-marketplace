# Release notes v6.14.0 🚀

**Date:** 2026-11-11\
**Type:** Minor\
**For whom:** consumers of this product

## What changed

Updating the plugins (`update-plugins`) is all most repos need to do. If your repo runs the core team,
there is one follow-up: two new specialists arrive, and you adopt them with `sync-roster`.

### Two new specialists

The core team grows by two. **Priya 🧩, the Product Owner**, writes user stories with checkable
acceptance criteria, orders the backlog and holds scope as a budget: something in means something out.
She proposes, you decide; Paula still owns *when*. **Uma 🖱️, the UX Designer**, prepares interviews and
usability tests, turns what comes back into findings tied to their evidence, and designs user flows,
wireframes and a clickable prototype. She never invents users: you run the session, she reads the result.

**What to do:** after updating, the roster check reports both as missing from your roster. Run
`sync-roster` once; it places an empty lens for each and proposes their roster lines.

### More from the team

- **Tycho** now also acts as QA lead. Hand him a release or a beta and you get a test plan, an
  acceptance check per criterion with evidence, and a go/no-go that names every open blocking bug. The
  release stays your call.
- **Cody** now also acts as tech lead. Ask him for an architecture decision record, a stack choice argued
  from your repo's constraints, or an estimate per story as a range with its assumptions.
- **Sebastian** can run a privacy (GDPR) check on a product that stores data about people: what it keeps
  and why, for how long, who processes it, and whether people can see, correct, export and delete it.
  Legal questions come back marked open for a lawyer, not answered.
- **Derek** is now titled **Repository Engineer** instead of DevOps Engineer, the work he already did.
  Your `SPECIALISTS.md` and his lens keep the old title until you edit them; nothing fails either way.

### Plugin updates stop stalling

`update-plugins -AllCheckouts` no longer skips a checkout because a plugin install rewrote
`.claude/settings.json` with another key order or a trailing newline. Such a rewrite is restored for
you, so that repo takes releases again without a hand `git restore`. A real edit is still left alone
and named.

### Smaller things

- A DEPLOY section that lost its audience heading is now refused at your own `open-pr`, with the heading
  to put back. Before, it passed and its audience score dropped silently out of the release.
- `build-handover-page.ps1` works from the installed plugin again, so a store repo builds its preview
  handover page without copying the plugin's files first.
- Shipped manuals now say "the owner" where they named a person. The rules themselves are unchanged.
