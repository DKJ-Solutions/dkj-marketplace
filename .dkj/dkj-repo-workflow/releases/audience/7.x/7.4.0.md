# Release notes v7.4.0 🚀

**Date:** 2026-11-23\
**Type:** Minor\
**For whom:** consumers of this product

## What changed

BWJ stores and Shopify repos may have one step to take after this update, and those steps come first
below. Every other repo needs no action. The rest makes Shopify runs fail fast, life hubs tidier, and
gives you two new skills.

### Action for some repos

- **BWJ stores: copy the Asana workflows again when you move their `ref:`.** The three Asana workflows
  (`asana-closed-message`, `asana-open-section`, `asana-prio-label`) now also fetch `scripts/lib`. When
  you move their `ref:` to this release, copy the three files from the plugin's `templates/` again and
  keep your own `ref:` line. Until you do, `closed-message-sessioncheck` names each workflow that differs
  from its template, and a workflow moved to the new `ref:` alone stops with a message that says exactly
  this.
- **Shopify Plus stores: answer your theme limit.** The theme slot checks now read the store's limit
  from `Get-ShopifyThemeLimit` in `scripts/repo-config.ps1`. It is 20 unless you answer it. On Shopify
  Plus, answer 100 to get the right free-slot count back. Until then, a store with more than 20 themes
  is told its limit looks wrong. Its live backup is no longer refused as full, and it is no longer told
  to delete themes.
- **Shopify repos with a live-theme branch: re-run Part 1 of `adopt-dkj-repo-workflow`.** Hotfix pull
  requests into your live branch now get the Branch entry check too, so a malformed changelog entry can
  no longer land on live unjudged. A repo adopted before this release is told the one trigger line to
  add.

### New

- **Start the Stopwatch in your own words.** "Use the stopwatch", "time this" or "gebruik de stopwatch"
  in your request now times that assignment, as does `/dkj-repo-workflow:stopwatch-start <label>`. Text
  you paste in, or a sentence that only talks about the stopwatch, starts nothing.
- **Check a tip before you build it.** Share a post, a link or a pasted text and ask whether it is worth
  it. The new `tip-check` skill holds the tip against your own setup, asks whether the problem actually
  happens to you, counts the cost, and advises Build, Later or Skip. Marlowe tries to prove that advice
  wrong before you see it.
- **Life hub dossiers keep their word count.** The new `dossier-wordcount` skill recounts a dossier
  after every write and names a page that crosses your split threshold. Create the
  `Instellingen dossier-wordcount` settings page in Notion once to set the property name and the
  threshold.
- **Your agenda run asks about doubles and clashes.** `agenda-sync` now flags a possible duplicate or a
  same-day clash as a question at the top of its report, and never moves or removes anything because of
  it. The first run adds a `Gesignaleerd` column to your Agenda database.

### Fixes you will notice

- **An expired Shopify login no longer hangs a run.** A release prep, live preflight, preview push,
  backup, archive or sweep stops within a minute when the Shopify CLI needs you to log in. It names the
  one command that logs you back in.
- **Dossiers read newest first.** Ian, `inbox-sweep`, `ai-radar` and `agenda` put an update at the top
  of its heading, so you find a dossier's latest state at the top.
- **Dossiers stay short.** A dossier is now a list of dated events. Long research goes to a document in
  your long-form folder, and the dossier gets one line with the conclusion and a link.
- **Each detail lives in one dossier.** A parent dossier introduces and links to its children instead
  of repeating their amounts and dates, so the hub no longer contradicts itself.
- **Shorter dossier names.** A new or renamed dossier repeats no word from higher up its path, so you
  get `Wedding › DJ` instead of `Wedding › DJ wedding`.
- **Files beside a branch document are caught.** A branch that commits screenshots or other files into
  a folder named after its branch document is now stopped at `open-pr` and by the Branch entry check.
  Before, that folder stayed on the trunk after the merge.
