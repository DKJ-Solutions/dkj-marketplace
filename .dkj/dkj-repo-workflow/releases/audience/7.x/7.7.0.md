# Release notes v7.7.0 🚀

**Date:** 2026-11-27\
**Type:** Minor\
**For whom:** consumers of this product

## What changed

Most repos need do nothing beyond updating. Four steps are optional and come first: refresh your
fold runner, run `sync-roster` in a life hub, and, in a BWJ store, re-place two templates. After
that come a new life-hub specialist, specialists that start from the best sources in their field,
and a round of fixes.

### Steps worth taking

- **Refresh your fold runner if you run the CI floor.** After `ship-pr` pushed its own fold commit,
  the runner for that merge could leave other changelog entries unfolded while reporting green. It now
  folds what is left, or fails red. Run `adopt-ci-floor` again to place the new runner. Until then,
  fold leftover entries by hand.
- **Run `sync-roster` in your life hub.** This brings in the lens for Rutger, the new Behavioral
  Analyst (below).
- **In a BWJ store, re-place `asana-closed-message`.** Linking an Asana task to an issue that is
  already closed now sends the closed message and moves the card into review, instead of a "now in
  progress" comment. `closed-message-sessioncheck` tells you when your copy is the old one.
- **In a BWJ store, copy the new resolves-gate template.** After a merge of an Asana-linked issue,
  your session now closes the issue itself with the `gh issue close` line the refusal prints. Copy the
  `Get-ResolvesExemptMatchers` answer from `adopt-dkj-repo-workflow-bwj` to get the new text.

### New for every team

- **Specialists start from the best sources.** Rebecca, Marlowe, Uma, Sebastian, Nolan, Rutger, Hugo
  and Fiona each carry a short canon of the sources that define their field, graded A to C. They look
  there first, then in your repo's own source store, and only then on the web.
- **Drafts are written as you.** A message the session drafts for you to send, such as an Asana
  comment or a reply, is now in the first person singular. You no longer rewrite "we" into "I".

### New for BWJ stores

- **Asana tickets have a front door.** The new `asana-intake` skill reads a colleague's Asana task,
  checks whether it is already filed or resolved, verifies the stated cause, and ends with one
  outcome. Any question for the requester is drafted in the chat, never posted.
- **Requests from chat link their task.** A new hook refuses an issue that names a requester but links
  no Asana task, and `report-issue` searches the board first. The colleague's card then moves to
  Filed and they hear when the work ships.
- **A missing live branch is reported.** If your branch-entry CI caller leaves out your live deploy
  branch, a session check now warns at every start and prints the line to use.

### New for life hubs

- **Meet Rutger, the Behavioral Analyst.** Rutger explains your patterns plainly: the observation, the
  mechanism with a source he opened and how solid it is, a competing explanation, and his confidence.
  He gives no advice and no diagnosis. Hugo advises; Rutger explains.
- **A stuck button.** The new `log-mental-block` skill helps you start when you have no drive, and
  keeps each moment in one Notion journal that shows patterns over weeks.
- **One source library.** A paper or study becomes one row in your hub's source library, never a
  dossier. Every specialist looks there before searching the web, so a source is found once.
- **Clearer placement.** Ian keeps every dossier name unique, places people in the ring of closeness
  you name, files tools by what they are used for, keeps one-off events out of reference catalogs,
  and files a feeling as an update, not a node of its own.
- **Short task cards.** A card's short note holds one line, what you have to do. Facts and links go
  in the card's page.

### Fixes you will notice

- **HTML-only mail is read.** The inbox sweep no longer files a mail whose plain text is only a "view
  online" stub as empty. It reads the HTML part instead.
- **Any spelling of your Gmail address works.** Dots, a `+tag` or `googlemail.com` no longer make the
  sweep stop or skip your own mail.
- **Your processing order wins.** Add an **order** row to the sweep's settings page to set it.
- **Cloud sessions can search before filing.** The duplicate search no longer fails with a 422 and no
  longer counts pull requests as duplicates.
- **Cloud sessions skip Windows-only checks.** `check-roster-sync` and `check-plugin-integrity` print
  a `[SKIP]` line on Linux instead of crashing or reporting platform noise.
- **Word counts match everywhere.** A session without PowerShell installs `pwsh` to run
  `dossier-wordcount`, instead of rebuilding the counter.
