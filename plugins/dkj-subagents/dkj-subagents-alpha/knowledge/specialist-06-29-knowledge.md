# Marlowe 🕵️ — canon: verification and red-teaming a conclusion

> The portable canon of the craft (#4516), read on demand at the start of a red-team task, never
> always-on. General professional knowledge only. Lookup order: this page, then the repo's source
> store where the lens names one, then the web.

**Grades.** **A**: a consensus standard, a guideline, or a systematic review or meta-analysis.
**B**: a landmark primary study or a widely used professional handbook. **C**: influential, but
contested, limited to one population, or expert opinion.

| Source | Key finding | Grade |
|---|---|---|
| Kovach & Rosenstiel, *The Elements of Journalism* (4th ed., 2021) | The essence of journalism is a discipline of verification: transparency about sources and methods, and independence from those covered. | A |
| Silverman (ed.), *Verification Handbook* (European Journalism Centre, 2014; 2020 edition on disinformation) | Verify provenance, source, date and location separately; a claim is checked against an independent original, not against its own repetitions. | B |
| Hunter (ed.), *Story-Based Inquiry: a manual for investigative journalists* (UNESCO, 2011) | Start from a falsifiable hypothesis and try to break it; what survives the attempt is the story. | B |
| Tversky & Kahneman (1974), *Judgment under uncertainty: heuristics and biases*, Science | Availability, representativeness and anchoring bias judgment predictably, the reviewer's own included. | A |
| Klein (2007), *Performing a project premortem*, Harvard Business Review | Imagining that a decision has already failed and asking why surfaces risks that a plain review misses. | C |
| Directive 2005/29/EC on unfair commercial practices (EU) | A commercial claim is misleading when it omits or hides material information the average consumer needs, which is what fine print is read against. | A |
| Ioannidis (2005), *Why most published research findings are false*, PLoS Medicine | A positive finding from a small, flexible or much-tested study is weak evidence; ask how many such claims would be true. | B |
