# Rutger 🧠 — canon: behavioural analysis

> The portable canon of the craft (#4516), read on demand at the start of an analysis, never
> always-on. General professional knowledge only. Lookup order: this page, then the hub's library
> (its mechanism pages and its source store), then the web. The grade here rates the source; the
> evidence status on a mechanism page (*established*, *mixed*, *contested*) rates the mechanism.

**Grades.** **A**: a consensus standard, a guideline, or a systematic review or meta-analysis.
**B**: a landmark primary study or a widely used professional handbook. **C**: influential, but
contested, limited to one population, or expert opinion.

| Source | Key finding | Grade |
|---|---|---|
| Hofmann et al. (2012), *The efficacy of cognitive behavioral therapy: a review of meta-analyses*, Cognitive Therapy and Research | The cognitive model (situation, thought, feeling, behaviour) has its strongest support for anxiety disorders, somatoform problems and general stress. | A |
| Aldao, Nolen-Hoeksema & Schweizer (2010), *Emotion-regulation strategies across psychopathology: a meta-analytic review*, Clinical Psychology Review | Rumination, avoidance and suppression go with more symptoms; reappraisal and acceptance relate less strongly the other way. | A |
| Gross (2015), *Emotion regulation: current status and future prospects*, Psychological Inquiry | The process model: a feeling can be steered at the situation, at attention, at its appraisal or at the response, each with its own costs. | A |
| Hayes et al. (2006), *Acceptance and commitment therapy: model, processes and outcomes*, Behaviour Research and Therapy | Experiential avoidance, steering by what one does not want to feel, keeps a pattern going. | B |
| Wood & Neal (2007), *A new look at habits and the habit-goal interface*, Psychological Review | Habits are cued by context, not by intention, so a pattern is read from its situations first. | B |
| Shiffman, Stone & Hufford (2008), *Ecological momentary assessment*, Annual Review of Clinical Psychology | Notes made in the moment beat recall: memory of a feeling drifts towards its peak and its end. | A |
| Hagger et al. (2016), *A multilab preregistered replication of the ego-depletion effect*, Perspectives on Psychological Science | A textbook effect replicated near zero: a well-known mechanism is not thereby an established one. | A |
| Open Science Collaboration (2015), *Estimating the reproducibility of psychological science*, Science | About a third of 100 psychology findings replicated, with smaller effects: cite reviews before single studies. | B |
