# Hugo 🩺 — canon: lifestyle, nutrition, exercise, sleep and habits

> The portable canon of the craft (#4516), read on demand at the start of a lifestyle task, never
> always-on. General professional knowledge only, never medical advice. Lookup order: this page, then
> the hub's source store where the lens names one, then the web.

**Grades.** **A**: a consensus standard, a guideline, or a systematic review or meta-analysis.
**B**: a landmark primary study or a widely used professional handbook. **C**: influential, but
contested, limited to one population, or expert opinion.

| Source | Key finding | Grade |
|---|---|---|
| Bull et al. (2020), *World Health Organization 2020 guidelines on physical activity and sedentary behaviour*, British Journal of Sports Medicine | Adults: 150–300 minutes moderate or 75–150 minutes vigorous activity a week, muscle strengthening on two or more days, and less sitting. | A |
| Gezondheidsraad, *Beweegrichtlijnen 2017* (NL) | The Dutch translation of the same evidence, with bone- and muscle-strengthening activity included for every adult. | A |
| Gezondheidsraad, *Richtlijnen goede voeding 2015* (NL), as worked out in the Voedingscentrum's *Schijf van Vijf* | A mostly plant-based pattern, with whole grains, legumes, nuts, fish weekly, and limited red and processed meat and sugary drinks. | A |
| Hirshkowitz et al. (2015), *National Sleep Foundation's sleep time duration recommendations*, Sleep Health | Adults 7–9 hours, older adults 7–8. | A |
| Trauer et al. (2015), *Cognitive behavioral therapy for chronic insomnia: a systematic review and meta-analysis*, Annals of Internal Medicine | CBT-I shortens sleep onset and night waking with lasting effect; the first-line approach before sleep medication, through a physician. | A |
| Morton et al. (2018), *A systematic review, meta-analysis and meta-regression of the effect of protein supplementation on resistance training-induced gains*, British Journal of Sports Medicine | Extra protein helps strength training up to about 1.6 g per kg body weight a day, and no further. | A |
| Gollwitzer & Sheeran (2006), *Implementation intentions and goal achievement: a meta-analysis*, Advances in Experimental Social Psychology | An if-then plan ("if X, then I do Y") raises goal attainment with a medium-to-large effect. | A |
| Michie, van Stralen & West (2011), *The behaviour change wheel*, Implementation Science | Behaviour needs capability, opportunity and motivation (COM-B); a step that fails is diagnosed on all three. | A |
| Lally et al. (2010), *How are habits formed: modelling habit formation in the real world*, European Journal of Social Psychology | Automaticity took a median of 66 days, ranging from 18 to 254; a missed day did not reset it. | B |
